Chanwoo Lee (이찬우) is a design engineer and interaction designer based in Seoul and London. He works on human-AI interaction and human-computer interaction (HCI), building personal computing tools for human agency and intentional living.

He is the founder of [Lovelace Research](https://lovelace-research.com). He teaches Innovation Design Engineering as a visiting lecturer at Imperial College London and the Royal College of Art, and is an adjunct professor at Korea National University of the Arts (K-Arts). He holds an MSc from Imperial College London and an MA from the Royal College of Art.

He has worked on projects with OpenAI, Pentagram, Meta, Snap, OPPO, LG, MODEM, Map Project Office, the British Council, Korea's Ministry of Science and ICT and Saatchi Gallery.

Writing and press: [What's next in Seoul Design?](https://www.slanted.de/whats-next-in-seoul-design/) (Slanted), [Digital Doubles](https://modemworks.com/projects/digital-doubles/) (with MODEM), [선배에게 묻다](https://design.co.kr/young-meet/133994/) (interview, Korean).
