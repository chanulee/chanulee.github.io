LG Intelligent System

A 3-part AI & connectivity R&D project focused on multi-device connectivity, a "SuperApp" strategy, and a long-term Human-AI Interaction roadmap. My role was to establish design principles for Human-AI interaction, design a hybrid AI interface, and create a data learning system and AI architecture blueprint for LG's future.

Our proposal for an AI-powered content organization feature was accepted and launched as a key feature in the 2024 LG gram Pro laptops.

Research & Development

+

The project was divided into three core areas: researching seamless multi-device connectivity, proposing a unified "SuperApp" strategy for LG's ecosystem, and developing a 2-3 year roadmap for Human-AI interaction systems.

Design & Impact

+

I structured the design principles and objectives for Human-AI interaction, focusing on a hybrid interface that blends user control with AI assistance. Using an interaction-centric AI approach, I helped streamline the data learning system and developed an AI architecture blueprint to guide LG's future AI roadmap.
