Sunny Day

An interactive mixed-reality system for immersive storytelling that provides a high degree of freedom for creative expression. The system integrates UWB sensors, Azure Kinect, and HTC Vive to create a seamless experience.

'Sunny Day' is a Solarpunk media art piece about a future where pixels approach the realm of atoms, and the climate crisis has been overcome. It explores themes of technological harmony with nature and optimistic futures.

Technical Details

+

The system is designed for immersive storytelling with high freedom of expression, integrating Ultra-Wideband (UWB) sensors for precise tracking, Azure Kinect for depth sensing and body tracking, and HTC Vive for virtual reality rendering. This combination allows for rich, interactive narratives.

Creative Vision

+

This work is a piece of Solarpunk media art, envisioning a future where the climate crisis is resolved and technology, represented by pixels, exists in harmony with the natural world, represented by atoms. It's an optimistic exploration of a sustainable future.

My Contribution

+

As the research team lead, I directed the research, design, and engineering of the MR system. My work included developing a wearable input device for intuitive interaction. As a co-artist, I contributed to the research on future scenarios and humanity's current climate technologies that informed the artwork's narrative.
