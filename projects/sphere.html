AI Sphere

People often miscommunicate with AI because they lack a deep understanding of the technology. Instead of forcing users to learn complex technical details, this project explores how we can foster empathy and mutual understanding between humans and AI.

The AI Sphere is an interactive artifact that communicates its internal state through non-verbal cues. For example, when a query requires significant processing time, the sphere breathes heavily, giving the user an intuitive sense of its "effort."

Design Philosophy

+

The core idea is to move beyond purely functional human-AI interaction and explore more empathetic and intuitive communication channels. By giving the AI a physical, expressive form, we can leverage human social cues to create a more natural and less frustrating user experience.

Hackathon Project

+

This project was developed for the Huawei Imperial Human-Machine Interaction Hackathon. The goal was to create innovative solutions for more natural and intuitive HMI. Our project was awarded 2nd place for its novel approach to empathetic AI interaction.
