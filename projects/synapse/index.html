TwelveLabs

Translating technology into metaphor and visual language for TwelveLabs, a cutting-edge AI company specializing in video understanding technology. The project focused on creating a visual identity that bridges the gap between complex machine learning concepts and human comprehension.

Working with Pentagram, we developed a comprehensive brand system that communicates the sophisticated yet accessible nature of AI-powered video analysis, making advanced technology feel approachable and understandable to diverse audiences.

Design Philosophy

+

The challenge was to create a visual language that could represent the complexity of AI video understanding while remaining accessible to both technical and non-technical audiences. We approached this by developing metaphors that relate machine learning processes to familiar human experiences.

The visual system draws inspiration from the concept of "seeing" and "understanding" - fundamental aspects of both human cognition and computer vision. Through careful typography, color, and imagery choices, we created a brand that feels both sophisticated and approachable.

Technical Implementation

+

The brand system was designed to be highly adaptable across digital and physical touchpoints. From web applications to conference presentations, the visual identity maintains consistency while allowing for contextual flexibility.

Key considerations included accessibility standards, cross-platform compatibility, and scalability for a rapidly growing technology company entering new markets and verticals.

Impact and Results

+

The rebrand helped TwelveLabs communicate their sophisticated AI capabilities more effectively to potential clients and partners. The visual language successfully bridges the gap between complex technology and clear communication, supporting the company's growth in the competitive AI market.
